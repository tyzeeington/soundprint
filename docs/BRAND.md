# SoundPrint

**Tagline:** "Reverse engineer any sound with your DAW's stock instruments"

## Brand Identity

**What it does:**
SoundPrint analyzes any sound and recreates it using stock instruments from your DAW (Ableton, FL Studio, Logic). Upload audio, paste links, or capture sounds directly from your DJ software - get back presets and instructions.

**Target Audience:**
- Music producers wanting to recreate sounds they hear
- DJs who hear sounds they love while mixing
- Bedroom producers learning sound design
- Anyone who's ever thought "how did they make that sound?"

**Tone:**
Light, accessible, vibey. Not intimidating or overly technical. Make sound design feel achievable.

**Key Features:**
1. Upload audio files (MP3, WAV)
2. Paste YouTube/Spotify links
3. Describe sounds with text ("warm analog bass")
4. Direct integration with rekordbox/serato while DJing
5. Get downloadable preset files (.adg, .fst, .logic)
6. Step-by-step instructions included

**Business Model:**
Freemium - 3 free recreations per month, then paid subscription for unlimited access

**Color Palette Ideas:**
- Primary: Deep purple/indigo (creative, musical)
- Accent: Bright cyan/electric blue (energy, tech)
- Background: Dark mode by default (producer-friendly)

## MVP Features (Phase 1)
- Landing page with email signup
- Audio file upload
- Text description input
- Basic sound analysis
- Ableton preset generation
- Email delivery of presets
